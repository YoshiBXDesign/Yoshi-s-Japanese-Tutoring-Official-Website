THE FRICTION

You Already Know Exactly What This Feels Like.

You can already picture the moments. The pressure is small. The hesitation is instant. The rest of the trip starts rearranging itself around avoidance.

  • You rehearse one simple question in your head, then say nothing when the moment arrives.
  • You default to apology before trying, even when the Japanese you need is basic and learnable.
  • You let your travel partner handle stations, restaurants, check-ins, and small corrections.
  • You over-rely on translation apps, then freeze when the real reply comes back too fast.
  • You return home with the same regret: “I was there, but I still stayed on the outside.”
THE SYSTEM

Not a Language Class.
A Confidence System.

This program is built for a narrow goal: making you meaningfully more usable in Japan before the trip begins. We are not trying to impress anyone. We are building reliable responses for real moments.

Situation-first training

Every session is anchored to travel situations you are statistically certain to face.

Spoken repetition under pressure

You will not just recognize phrases. You will practice using them while slightly uncomfortable.

Repair language

You will learn what to say after you miss something, mishear something, or need help buying time.

Travel-specific judgment

You will learn when Japanese helps, when English is fine, and how to move between the two without panic.

Active Participation Required.

THE SYLLABUS

8 Sessions.
6 Hours.
Every Real Situation, Covered.

8 live sessions. 45 minutes each. Structured for the traveler who wants enough language to move, ask, correct, recover, and connect.

Session 1 Your First 24 Hours in Japan

Room Survival & Asking for Help Without Guilt

Your first interaction in Japan does not require perfection. This session gives you one phrase that works as a universal key across every situation on day one, and the exact check-in script that makes arrival feel like arrival, not survival.

Session 2 Never Freeze at the Ticket Gate

Transit, Navigation & Crowds

Station staff across Japan are trained for exactly this moment. One grammatical pattern, applied to any destination, turns the most intimidating transit system in the world into a network you can navigate, confirm, and recover from with confidence.

Session 3 Shop & Pay Like You Belong There

Conbini, Shopping & Payment

The convenience store is not a transaction. It is a daily, low-stakes environment where real Japanese interactions happen at your own pace, and this session gives you the complete script for every counter moment, from payment to politely declining a bag.

Session 4 Order Food With Confidence

Cafe & Restaurant Survival

The local restaurant you have been walking past is waiting for you. This session turns one word, "omakase," into your most confident order: a gesture that removes menu anxiety entirely and communicates respect to the chef in the same breath.

Session 5 Eat Your Way Without Shame

Dietary Restrictions & Group Dining

Asking about food in Japan is not an inconvenience. You will leave this session with a precise, unambiguous template for stating what you cannot eat and asking what something is, in a way that reads as genuine curiosity, not helplessness.

Session 6 Connect With Locals Naturally

Small Talk & Making Friends

The izakaya has its own language, and knowing the ritual is what separates a tourist watching from the corner from a person genuinely at the table. This session covers the full sequence from first order to kanpai, and the few phrases that convert a shared drink into an actual conversation.

Session 7 What To Say When Things Go Wrong

Emergency & Tech Failures

This is the session most travelers hope they will not need and cannot afford to skip. You will leave with a clear, tested template for communicating pain, location, and distress, so that when technology fails, you do not.

Session 8 Travel Japan With Confidence

Integration & Real-world Simulation

Seven sessions of practical language become one coherent identity. This final session integrates everything through real-scenario role-play, reframes every mistake as fluency in progress, and leaves you with something no phrasebook can provide: the quiet confidence that you can handle whatever comes.

THE FAQ

What Most Serious Applicants Ask.

When does the program start?

There are no fixed start dates. Instead, students are grouped based on their travel schedules. Once enough travelers with similar timelines are matched, you'll receive your class schedule.

Do I need to know Japanese before joining?

No. The program is designed for complete beginners and first-time visitors to Japan.

What if my travel date changes?

Let us know as early as possible. We'll do our best to place you in a later group that better matches your new schedule.

Why is the Founding Member Program only $50?

Founding Members help us improve the program through detailed feedback after the course. In return, they receive the complete program at a significantly reduced price.

Is this a private lesson?

No. This is a live small-group program where you'll practice real travel situations with other travelers.
