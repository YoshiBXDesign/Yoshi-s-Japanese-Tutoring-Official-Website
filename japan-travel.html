THE SYSTEM

Not a Language Class.
A Confidence System.

This program is built for a narrow goal: making you meaningfully more usable in Japan before the trip begins. We are not trying to impress anyone. We are building reliable responses for real moments.

Situation-first training

Every session is anchored to travel situations you are statistically certain to face.

Spoken repetition under pressure

You will not just recognize phrases. You will practice using them while slightly uncomfortable.

Repair language

Travel-specific judgment

You will learn when Japanese helps, when English is fine, and how to move between the two without panic.

THE SYLLABUS

8 Sessions. 6 Hours. Every Real Situation, Covered.

8 live sessions. 45 minutes each. Structured for the traveler who wants enough language to move, ask, correct, recover, and connect.

  1. 01

    The Mindset Reset

    How to stop performing and start using simple Japanese before fear interrupts you.

  2. 02

    Arrival, Check-In, and First Contact

    Airports, hotels, simple requests, timing questions, and the first ten minutes that set the tone.

  3. 03

    Stations Without Panic

    Platform questions, transfer confusion, ticket problems, and asking for help when you are already late.

  4. 04

    Restaurants, Cafes, and Counters

    Ordering, preferences, allergies, sold-out items, and what to do when the script breaks.

  5. 05

    Shopping, Payment, and Everyday Friction

    Sizes, stock, tax-free questions, card issues, and the language of ordinary transactions.

  6. 06

    When You Need Help Fast

    Being lost, clarifying directions, handling misunderstandings, and staying composed under social pressure.

  7. 07

    Talking to Real People

    Short conversations with hosts, staff, acquaintances, and family members without shutting down.

  8. 08

    Full Simulation and Personal Repair

    Your likely trip scenarios, your weak points, and the exact phrases you need stabilized before departure.
