METHOD

A Method Built Around Use, Not Just Study.

We begin by looking closely at how you learn now. From there, we build practice around the moments where you need to use Japanese. Then we refine it through what happens when you use it in real life.

01 — DECONSTRUCT

See How You Are Learning Now.

We begin with more than a textbook or a level label. Together, we look at your current habits, the situations where Japanese still feels uncertain, and the gaps between what you understand and what you can actually use.

This gives us a clearer starting point: what is already working, what needs attention, and where Japanese needs to become part of your life beyond the lesson.

02 — REBUILD

Build Practice Around Real Use.

We identify the conversations, responsibilities, and routines where Japanese needs to show up in your life. Then we shape lessons and practice around those moments, not around material to complete for its own sake.

The work may include speaking, writing, preparation, and review. What matters is that each part gives you something you can carry into the next real situation.

03 — STABILIZE

Make Japanese More Dependable in Real Life.

Progress is shaped between sessions. You use Japanese, notice where it breaks down, and bring those moments back into the work.

My role is to take those attempts and refine them into clearer, more natural language. Each cycle of use, feedback, and revision makes your Japanese more dependable under real conditions.
